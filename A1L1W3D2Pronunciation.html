What's the same everywhere

Where you see Record, you record your own voice and listen back. Where you see Practise speaking, the computer shows the words it heard so you can compare them with the model — it never listens to your accent and never gives you a mark.

AI feedback receives only the recognised words. Your Record and compare audio stays in this browser and is deleted when you leave the screen.

Le retour IA reçoit uniquement les mots reconnus. L'enregistrement reste dans ce navigateur et est supprimé lorsque vous quittez l'écran.
