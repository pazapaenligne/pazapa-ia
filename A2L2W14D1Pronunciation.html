Pronunciation Clinic

What happened?

You practise three sentences about an accident. You read each sentence. You say it in two parts, with a small pause between the parts.

Practise speaking

For each sentence, record your voice and practise speaking before you carry on. You must record yourself on every sentence.

This is AI-assisted speaking feedback. The computer reads your words. It does not listen to your accent. It does not give you a mark.

Well done

You read and practised all three sentences about what happened.

You can tell a short story with a small pause: “I was driving / when it happened.”

Say the sentences again at home. Use the speaking chunks and stop for a moment between the two parts.
