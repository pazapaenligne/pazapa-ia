Pronunciation Clinic

Making Arrangements

You will practise saying useful English for making arrangements: asking if someone is free, saying you are busy, and agreeing on a time.

Listen, notice, and record your own voice. There is no score — this is practice for you.

Vous allez vous entraîner à prononcer des phrases utiles pour prendre rendez-vous. Il n'y a pas de note : c'est un entraînement personnel.

Screen 1 of 6

Listen and notice

A question ending in "yes/no" usually rises at the end. A reply that agrees usually stays level or falls.

Are you available on Monday?
That works for me.

Listen to each line, then say it yourself and notice your own voice rise on the question and settle on the reply.
