Pronunciation Clinic

A small disagreement

The noise next door is too loud. How do you ask politely, without sounding angry?

You will practise one request. You will say it, record it and listen to it.

Could you keep the noise down, please?

Notice how it is said

Two neighbours say the same request. One is rushed. One is measured. Look at the marks and choose the measured one.

This choice is about the marks you can see. The marks are teaching notes, not a sound test.

Rehearse in small chunks

Say each phrase out loud in its chunks. Then record yourself. The boxed chunk is the beat to lift and soften. You need one recording for each phrase.

    Recording studio

    Could you keep the noise down, please?

    Keep an even pace. Take a small pause after “you”. Let “please” end gently. Many speakers lift their voice a little on “please”. There is no single correct pitch.
