Pronunciation Clinic

What did you do yesterday?

It is Monday morning. Hana asks Mariam about yesterday. Listen to the end of each past verb. You read, say and record six short sentences.

The conversation

Hana: “What did you do yesterday?”

Mariam: “I worked until six. Then I cooked dinner. I was tired.”

Worked, cooked and watched end with a t sound. Stayed ends with a d sound. Visited has an extra syllable. You do not add an extra syllable to the others.

Well done

You practised clear past-tense endings in a short message about yesterday.

Worked, cooked and watched end with a t sound. Stayed ends with a d sound. Visited has an extra syllable.

Say your message again at home: “I worked until six. Then I cooked dinner.”
